Artificial Intelligence is transforming the world. Machine learning models can now process text, images, and audio. Large language models like Claude are trained on vast amounts of data. RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation. It combines a vector database with a language model. The vector database stores chunks of text as embeddings. When a user asks a question, the most relevant chunks are retrieved. Those chunks are then passed to the LLM to generate an answer. This makes LLMs more accurate and grounded in specific documents.
